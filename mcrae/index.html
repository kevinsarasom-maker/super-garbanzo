06B / The capture standard

Perspective

Same lens families, same eight angles, same product-to-frame proportions. Front-on frames are square to the graphic with a level camera and corrected verticals. Site one becomes the visual reference.

Photo baseline

Tripod, RAW, manual exposure, ISO 100, f/8 as a starting point. Bracket the lightbox so both the graphic and the room hold detail.

Colour & light

Grey card at every site, custom white balance per lighting setup, polarizer for glare. The graphic never clips to white.

Motion

4K, locked white balance, 30 fps at 1/60 with 60 fps slow-motion details. Test LED flicker before every setup. Horizontal and vertical takes are recorded separately.
