06 · The capture standard

Match the result.
Record the setup.

Working technical starting points, confirmed against Kevin’s camera system and the actual displays at the scout. Consistency means matching colour, perspective, motion and finish, with controlled adjustments for each space.

PhotographyRAWTripod, manual exposure, ISO 100, f/8 starting point, 1/60 s for illuminated displays. Bracket static installs. Log lens, height and distance at site one.
ColourGrey cardAt every site, with a custom white balance per lighting set-up. No auto WB, no single Kelvin forced across spaces.
Motion4K29.97/30 fps at ~1/60 shutter; slow-motion details at 59.94/60 fps at ~1/120, and high-frame-rate for the loop. LED flicker tested before every set-up.
Orientation16:9 + 9:16Horizontal and vertical passes recorded separately, matching movement direction, speed and colour.
