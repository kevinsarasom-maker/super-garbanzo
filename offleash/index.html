    Common questions

    Ask the dog

    What's the difference between the three tiers?

    They all work the same way: a gallery of the whole shoot lands in your inbox that night, you heart the ones you love, and only those get hand-edited. The difference is how many you keep. Tier 1 ($700): 40 minutes, 15 images. Tier 2 ($1,000): 1 hour, 25 images. Tier 3 ($1,500): 1 hour, 35 images, extra edits at $25 instead of $50, plus every RAW frame (2,000 to 4,000 of them) online forever.

    My dog won't sit still, or gets nervous in new places.

    Totally normal. No perfect behavior required. Kevin works fast, at your dog's pace, keeps things calm, and photographs their real personality.

    Does my dog have to be on a leash?

    No. The studio is a safe, enclosed space, so your dog can be fully off leash. Any time a leash is needed, it gets removed in editing.

    How does payment work?

    A 50% deposit reserves your session. The balance is due on shoot day by cash, e-transfer, or PayPal. Framed wall art is sold separately.

    What if I need to reschedule?

    The deposit is non-refundable, but life happens. Give at least 48 hours' notice and you'll be moved to another date. Sessions are in-studio, so weather never cancels.

    Can I bring more than one dog?

    Bring the whole crew. Each extra dog is a small add-on; mention it when you sign up.

    What should I bring?

    Their favorite treats (something to lick, plus small ones for tossing) and any props: a well-loved toy, a bandana, a special outfit.

    Do you photograph other pets?

    Yes, all pets are welcome. There's a hairless cat in the gallery to prove it.

    Do you offer gift cards?

    Yes, in any amount. Email kevin@offleashstudio.com to set one up.
