      How to play

      A Big Day is a real birding challenge: record as many species as you can in one day.

      Controls

      W A S D
      Walk. Arrow keys work too.
      Mouse
      Look around. Click the view first to capture the mouse.
      Shift
      Run. Fast, but it flushes birds.
      C
      Sneak. Slow and quiet, and it steadies the camera.
      Right mouse / Q
      Raise the camera. Hold, or press Q to toggle.
      Scroll / [ ]
      Zoom from 2× to 16×.
      Left mouse / Space
      Take a photo.
      E
      Identify the song you just heard.
      G / Esc
      Field guide / pause.

      Photographing

      Put the focus square on a bird and wait for it to turn green, then shoot. Photos score up to three stars for size in the frame, focus, sharpness, composition and a clear side-on view.

      Every bird has a comfort distance. Running doubles it. Sneaking roughly halves it, so crouch as you get close.

      Birding by ear

      Songs show up on the compass. Press E after a song and pick the singer from three birds. Real Big Days count heard birds too.
